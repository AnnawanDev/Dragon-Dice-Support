# Privacy Policy for Dragon Dice

**Last Updated: November 11, 2026**

Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Dragon Dice** handles information when you use our iOS mobile application. 

The short answer is: **We don't collect, store, or share your personal data.** We do collect anonymous usage data, such as which dice get rolled most, to help us understand how the app is used and to improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings (see Section 3).

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Dragon Dice is built with a "privacy by design" philosophy. 
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location).
* **No Account Creation:** You do not need to create an account or log in to use any features of the app.
* **No Tracking:** We do not use any advertising networks or tracking software that monitor your behavior across the web or other apps, and we never link app usage data to your identity.
* **Anonymous Usage Data:** We use a privacy-focused analytics service, TelemetryDeck, to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data. This is described in detail in Section 3.

## 2. On-Device Storage
Any data generated within the app—such as your custom character settings, dice preferences, or roll history—is stored **locally on your own iOS device**. 
* Your saved settings and roll history are never uploaded or backed up to our servers.
* The only exception is the anonymous usage statistics described in Section 3, which you can turn off in Settings.
* If you delete the app, this locally stored data will be permanently removed from your device.

## 3. Anonymous Usage Analytics (TelemetryDeck)
Dragon Dice uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), a privacy-first analytics service, to help us understand how the app is used—for example, which dice get rolled most and which features are popular—so future updates focus on what matters at your table.

* **What is sent:** Anonymous usage signals, including:
  * **Rolls:** the type of die rolled (such as D6 or D20), the number of dice, the modifier, and the roll result.
  * **Features and settings:** which features and settings are used, such as your chosen Tavern Hero, Shake to Roll, Stay Awake, and whether the tip screen is opened or a tip is made.
  * **Technical information:** app version, iOS version, device model, and language/region settings.
* **What is not sent:** We do not send your name, email address, Apple ID, location, contacts, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information.
* **How to opt out:** You can turn off anonymous usage statistics at any time in the app's **Settings**. Once turned off, no further usage data is sent from your device.
* **Anonymized identifiers:** To count unique users without identifying them, TelemetryDeck uses a randomly generated identifier that is hashed (and salted) before it leaves your device and hashed again on TelemetryDeck's servers. Neither we nor TelemetryDeck can use it to identify you or to track you across other apps or websites.
* **No IP address storage:** TelemetryDeck does not store your IP address.
* **No advertising or selling:** This data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never combined with data from other sources.
* **Aggregated reporting:** We only view usage data in aggregate (for example, totals and trends), not on a per-person basis.

For more information, please see [TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

## 4. Optional Tips (In-App Purchases)
Dragon Dice offers optional tips in Settings to support future development. Tips do not unlock any features.
* Tips are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID.
* We never receive or store your name, payment method, or billing details. Apple only tells us that a purchase was completed.
* Apple handles all refund requests. You can request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
* Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Other Third-Party Services
Besides a link to our X.com page, Apple's in-app purchase system, and the TelemetryDeck analytics described above, Dragon Dice does not include links to external websites, third-party frameworks, or SDKs that collect data, outside of standard Apple App Store services (such as anonymous crash reports provided directly by Apple if you have opted-in to share them with developers).

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because our application does not collect any personal information from any users—only the anonymous usage data described above—we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app updates or changes in legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
